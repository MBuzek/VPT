# HR *(Restricted)*

**Purpose:** Employee and personnel records. Sensitive by definition — access is
tightly limited.

**Access:** **Restricted** — Owners (Natalie, Alex) + HR department only.
General staff have **no visibility** into this site.

> HR *policy* decisions (what's retained, who's employed, personnel actions) are
> outside the architecture scope and belong to HR/leadership. This site defines
> only *where* HR records live and *who can reach them*.

## What does NOT belong here
