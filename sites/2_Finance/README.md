# Finance

**Purpose:** All active financial records — banking, reconciliations, revenue,
reporting, and audit support. This is a primary audit-response site, so naming
discipline matters most here.

**Access:** Departmental contribute (Finance staff) + view-only for other
non-restricted tiers per the access model.

## Sub-headings (defined)

- **Bank & Reconciliations** — bank statements, reconciliations by account/period
- **Revenue & Reporting** — revenue reports, management reporting
- **Audits & CPA** — audit workpapers, CPA correspondence, support packages

## Migration mapping (examples)

| Current source | New home |
|----------------|----------|
| `Accounting/Audits` | Finance / Audits & CPA |
| Bank statements & recons | Finance / Bank & Reconciliations |
| Revenue/management reports | Finance / Revenue & Reporting |

## What belongs here

- Active reconciliations, statements, revenue and management reports
- Current-year and recent-prior-year audit support

## What does NOT belong here

- Long-inactive historical financials → Legacy / Archive
- Personal working files → OneDrive
