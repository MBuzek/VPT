# All Company

**Purpose:** Company-wide shared content every employee can reach — the one site
general staff have routine (view) access to.

**Access:** All staff — **view-only** by default. Contribute limited to Owners /
Site Managers so it stays curated and doesn't become a dumping ground.

## Sub-headings — PENDING manager input

Recommended starting structure (confirm before migration):

- **Announcements & News**
- **Company Policies (general)**
- **Shared Resources & Templates**
- **Directory & Org Info**

## What belongs here

- Company-wide announcements and general policies
- Shared, non-sensitive resources and templates everyone uses

## What does NOT belong here

- Anything sensitive or department-restricted → the relevant function site
- Personal files → OneDrive
- **No personal folders here** — this is shared, curated content only
