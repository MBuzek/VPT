Dear Ms. Baca:

Thank you for your office's patience and guidance in resolving Vantage Point Title, Inc.'s 2025 reporting. We write to respectfully request waiver, or in the alternative mitigation, of any penalty associated with the late submission of our 2025 AECPR. We acknowledge the report was received after the May 15, 2026 deadline, and we want to be transparent about why, and what we have done to ensure it does not recur.

**The cause was an internal personnel transition, not disregard of the obligation.** The report itself was timely prepared by our independent CPA, Ed Hill & Associates. The employee responsible for submission departed the company and had marked this filing as complete on her off-boarding checklist. The completed report was, in fact, sitting in her email — it had been prepared, but not transmitted to your office. The gap was an oversight created by the transition, not a failure to perform the underlying compliance work.

**We cured immediately upon notice.** Within 24 hours of your office identifying the missing AECPR on June 9, 2026, we located the completed report and arranged its submission through Compliance Freedom (Kenneth Nickel) on June 10, 2026, which your office acknowledged as received and complete on June 11, 2026. We also confirmed the 2025 ASR was already on file with your office.

**We have closed the underlying control gap.** Vantage Point has consolidated all state filing obligations — including the NM ASR and AECPR — into a centralized compliance calendar with redundant ownership and advance reminders, so that no single individual's departure can again cause a missed transmittal.

Given that the report was prepared on time, that the delay resulted from a one-time personnel transition rather than neglect, that we cured within 24 hours of notice, and that this is a first occurrence, we respectfully request that the penalty be waived. Should the Office determine a penalty is nonetheless warranted, we ask that it be mitigated in recognition of these facts and our full cooperation.

We appreciate your consideration and remain available at your convenience.
